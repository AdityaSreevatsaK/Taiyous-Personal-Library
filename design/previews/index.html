CHAPTER 832

The Distant Summit

The first light of dawn touched the mountains beyond the mist. Beneath an endless sky, the young cultivator stood in silence and looked towards the distant summit.

The road had never promised to be easy. Every step had demanded patience, each trial a choice, and every victory had revealed the shape of another, greater world beyond.

He breathed in the cold mountain air. The pathways of his cultivation remained uncertain, but the determination that had carried him this far was steady as ever.

When the sun rose above the clouds, the valley turned to gold. Beyond the horizon lay a thousand possibilities, and for the first time in many days, he smiled.

The mountains did not move. It was he who had changed.
